Delete your Escuela360 account

App: Escuela360  |  Developer: Idea Locus Solutions, Kampala, Uganda

This page explains how to ask for your Escuela360 account and the data linked to it to be deleted.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to info@idealocussolutions.com with the subject Delete my Escuela360 account.
  2. Include the name and email address (or phone number) you use to sign in, and the name of your school.
  3. We may reply to confirm that the request really comes from you.
  4. We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email when it is done.

Parents, teachers, and students can also ask their school administrator to remove their account.

Request deletion of some data only

If you want specific data removed without closing your account (for example, a phone number or a profile photo), use the same email address and say which data you want deleted.

What is deleted

  • Your account profile: name, email address, phone number, and role
  • Your sign-in credentials
  • Personal details and files linked to your account
  • Notifications and messages sent to or from your account

What may be kept

  • School records that the school is required to keep, such as grades, attendance, and fee payment records. These belong to the school, and the school decides how long to keep them.
  • Information we must keep to follow the law or settle disputes.
  • Backups, which are removed within 30 days after the deletion.
