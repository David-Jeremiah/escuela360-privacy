Privacy Policy for Escuela360

Effective date: October 16, 2026
Operator: Idea Locus Solutions, Kampala, Uganda
Contact: info@idealocussolutions.com

1. Introduction

Escuela360 ("the app", "we", "us") is a school management app developed by Idea Locus Solutions. It helps schools manage students, teachers, parents, attendance, grades, fees, and communication. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, role (administrator, teacher, parent, or student), and login credentials.
  • School and student records entered by the school: student names, class, admission numbers, attendance, grades and report cards, fee records, and parent or guardian contact details.
  • Device and usage information: device type, operating system version, app version, crash reports, and basic usage data.
  • Camera: used only to scan QR codes, for example for attendance or ID verification. We do not store photos from the camera unless a feature clearly says so.
  • Documents: PDF files generated or printed from within the app, such as report cards or receipts.

3. How we use information

  • To create and manage accounts and provide the app's features.
  • To record attendance, grades, and fees.
  • To send notifications and messages related to school activities.
  • To improve performance, fix bugs, and keep the app secure.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who controls the data

Schools that use Escuela360 decide what student and staff data is entered. The school is responsible for having the right to collect that data, including consent from parents or guardians for students under 18. Idea Locus Solutions processes the data on behalf of the school.

5. Third-party services

The app uses trusted third-party services to operate, including Google Firebase (authentication, database, storage, and notifications). These services process data under their own privacy policies, available at policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Data storage and security

Data is stored on secure servers provided by Google Firebase and is protected with encryption in transit and access controls. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Data retention

We keep data for as long as the school's account is active or as needed to provide the service. When a school closes its account or requests deletion, data is deleted within 30 days unless the law requires us to keep it.

8. Children's privacy

Escuela360 is intended for school administrators, teachers, and parents or guardians. Student information is entered and managed by the school or parent, not collected directly from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without school or parental authorization. If you believe a child's data was added without permission, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Under Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, you may request to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or object to its processing. To do so, email us at info@idealocussolutions.com. Parents can also contact their child's school.

10. Account and data deletion

To delete your account and associated data, email info@idealocussolutions.com with the subject "Delete my account", or contact your school administrator. We will respond within 30 days.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, and the effective date will be updated.
